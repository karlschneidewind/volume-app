Privacy Policy

Volume for iPhone · Last updated 12 October 2026

In short: Volume never sends anything you enter to anyone. Your entries stay on your iPhone. The app has no account, no server and no internet connection, and the developer cannot see what you log.

What this policy covers

The Volume app for iPhone, this website, and emails you send to the developer. You never have to provide personal data: the app works without it, and writing an email is up to you.

Who is responsible

Karl Schneidewind, karl.swd@proton.me

What the app stores, and where

Your workouts, exercises (with their muscle groups), sets, daily nutrition totals, body-weight entries and targets, and the app’s settings, such as its language and appearance. Some of this, such as body weight and nutrition, is health-related. All of it stays on your iPhone, in the app’s own storage, and is processed only there, under your control. The developer has no access to it. If your iPhone has a passcode, iOS keeps this data encrypted while the iPhone is locked.

What the developer receives

Nothing you enter. Volume makes no internet connections. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, and no third-party components.

Apple

You get Volume from the App Store, which Apple runs under its own privacy policy. Apple gives the developer download and sales figures. If you have turned on “Share With App Developers” in iOS (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple also shares crash reports and usage statistics. Apple states that this information does not identify you, and it never contains anything you entered in the app.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, that backup includes Volume’s data. Apple or your computer handles the backup; the developer never sees it.

Exports and imports

Under Settings → Data you can export your data as a file. Volume creates the file and opens the iOS share sheet; where the file goes next is up to you. The app deletes its own temporary copy when you leave the Data screen, or at the latest the next time it starts. An import reads only the file you choose.

Deleting your data

You can delete single entries in the app, or all entries at once under Settings → Data → Erase all data. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone, including its internal safety copies. Backups of your iPhone and exports you made keep their copies until you delete them.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub B.V. (Netherlands) and GitHub, Inc. (USA), and delivered through GitHub’s content-delivery network. When you open a page, your browser sends your IP address and the usual request details. GitHub logs the IP address for security purposes and processes it as an independent controller under its own privacy statement, which also says how long data is kept. The developer has no access to these logs. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which covers processing in the USA. This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other services. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: the developer’s legitimate interest in providing information about the app, including this policy.

If you write to the developer

If you email the developer, your address and message are used only to answer you. They are deleted once your enquiry is settled, and no later than twelve months after your last message. The mailbox is hosted by Proton AG, Switzerland, a country the EU recognises as providing adequate data protection. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: the developer’s legitimate interest in answering your enquiry.

No automated decisions

No decisions are made about you automatically, and no profile of you is built.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your personal data (Art. 15), to have it corrected (Art. 16) or erased (Art. 17), to restrict its processing (Art. 18), to receive it in a portable format (Art. 20), and to complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77), for example the one where you live. The app never sends the developer anything, so for the app there is nothing to request; these rights matter for emails and for this website.

Your right to object (Art. 21 GDPR). Where data is processed on the basis of a legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), as for this website and for emails, you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. Write to karl.swd@proton.me. For GitHub’s logs you can also object to GitHub directly.

Health

Volume is a logbook. It gives no medical, nutritional or training advice, and its calculated figures, such as an estimated one-rep maximum, are only estimates. For decisions about your health, talk to a doctor or another qualified professional.

Children

Volume is not aimed at children, and the developer receives no data from anyone who uses it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date. If Volume ever starts sending data anywhere, this policy will say so before that version is released. The English and German versions say the same.
