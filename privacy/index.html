Privacy Policy

Volume for iPhone · Last updated 7 October 2026

In short: Volume collects nothing. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. The app has no account, no server and no network code, and the developer never receives any of your data.

Who is responsible

Karl Schneidewind, karl.swd@proton.me

What the app stores, and where

Your workouts, exercises (with their muscle groups), sets, daily nutrition totals, body-weight entries and targets, and the app’s settings, such as its language and appearance. All of it is stored on your iPhone, in the app’s own storage, protected by iOS data protection.

What the developer receives

Nothing. Volume makes no network connections. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, and no third-party components. Nobody but you can see what you enter.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, that backup includes Volume’s data. Apple or your computer handles the backup; the developer never sees it.

Exports and imports

Under Settings → Data you can export your data as a file. Volume writes the file and opens the iOS share sheet; where the file goes next is up to you, and the app deletes its own temporary copy. An import reads only the file you choose.

Deleting your data

You can delete single entries in the app, or everything at once under Settings → Data → Erase all data. Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. Backups and exports you made keep their copy.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you open a page, your browser sends GitHub your IP address and the usual request details. GitHub uses them to deliver the page and to keep its service secure, and may store them in its logs; this can involve processing in the USA. The developer has no access to these logs. This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other services. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, a legitimate interest in providing information about the app. Details are in GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you write to the developer

If you email the developer, your address and message are used only to answer you and are kept only as long as that takes. The mailbox is hosted by Proton AG, Switzerland. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, a legitimate interest in answering your enquiry.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can request access to your personal data, and its correction, erasure or restriction; you can object to its processing and ask for a portable copy. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority. The app never sends the developer anything, so the developer holds no data from it, but you are welcome to ask at any time.

Health

Volume is a logbook. It gives no medical, nutritional or training advice, and its calculated figures, such as estimated one-rep maxima, are estimates. For decisions about your health, talk to a qualified professional.

Children

Volume is not aimed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date. If Volume ever starts sending data anywhere, this policy will say so before that version is released.
