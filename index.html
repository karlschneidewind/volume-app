Volume

A private training log for iPhone

Volume records what you lifted, what you ate and what you weigh. No account, no cloud, no subscription: your data stays on your iPhone.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: karl.swd@proton.me. If something isn’t working, please include your iOS version and the app version shown under Settings → About.

Common questions

Where is my data?
Only on your iPhone. Nobody else, including the developer, has a copy.

How do I back it up?
Settings → Data → Export JSON backup, then save the file somewhere other than the phone, such as iCloud Drive or a computer. An iCloud Backup of the iPhone includes Volume’s data too.

How do I move to a new iPhone?
Restoring a backup of the old iPhone brings Volume’s data with it. Or export a JSON backup on the old phone and choose Settings → Data → Import a backup on the new one.

How do I change the language?
Settings → Language: “System” follows your iPhone, or choose English or Deutsch.

How do I delete everything?
Settings → Data → Erase all data. It cannot be undone, so export a backup first if you might want your history back.
